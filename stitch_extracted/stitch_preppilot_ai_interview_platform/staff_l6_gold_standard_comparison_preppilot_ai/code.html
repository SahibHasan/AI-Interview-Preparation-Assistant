"Alex, your algorithmic logic in EventSinkBuffer is clean for an L5 interview, but in high-throughput tier-1 ingestion (50,000+ events/sec), V8 handles Map<number, T> by dynamically rehashing entry tables. Notice the 68ms pause during teardown: because the shutdown loop synchronously polls has() without yielding to the Node.js event loop, incoming cluster heartbeats get starved, triggering spurious pod kill signals.
